How we keep it
confidential

We never report a group smaller than five people. If only three assistants answer, that section of your report stays blank and says why. This is the rule we will not bend, and it is why staff tell us the truth.

Answers come to us, not to you

Staff respond on our system. The practice never sees a single response.

Comments are checked by a person

We remove names and details that would identify who wrote a comment before anything is shared.

Raw data stays with us

We keep individual responses for twelve months, then delete them. We do not sell them.
